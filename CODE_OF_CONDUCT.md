# Code of conduct

Contributors and maintainers must treat one another respectfully, welcome different experiences and perspectives, and focus criticism on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and unwanted sexual attention are unacceptable.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, reviews and project events. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation, apply proportionate enforcement, and protect reporters from retaliation. Maintainers are subject to the same rules.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an available private GitHub contact channel. Do not disclose private information in public issues. A dedicated confidential reporting route and additional governance procedures must be established before public launch (M10-07).
